As I clerked him, he told me his story.

A prisoner.  
A former miner.  
A father of nine girls.  
Only 41.

He had come with his first decompensation. Grade 3 ascites so tense that it made breathing difficult when sleeping.

He had been prescribed medication in the past he said..  He had not been taking it.

We tapped him. He breathed easier. He left well.

Later, as I read about cirrhosis, I came across the numbers: nine years often become 1.6 for most patients.[^1]

Optimal care, means more time.

That is why care cannot end with prescribing just the right medicine. It must ensure that the patient can, and does, take it.
